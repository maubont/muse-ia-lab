# Design System: Muse IA Lab — Luminous Champagne Gold & Soft Pastel Rose Atelier (v9.0 Zero-Purple)
**Project ID:** `muse-ia-lab-v9-champagne-rose`
**Skills Applied:** `design-md`, `frontend-app-builder`, `stitch-loop`

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
- **Aesthetic Philosophy:** *2026 Luminous Editorial Luxury & Candid iPhone Hyperrealism*. Inspired by **Dior Backstage, Rhode, Glossier Editorial, Apple Warm Titanium, and Swiss Gallery Minimalism**, combined with the high-conversion offer mechanics of `hypermodels.io/offer`.
- **Anti-Cliché Mandate (0% AI Purple & 0% Plastic Skin):**
  1. **Zero Purple:** Complete elimination of 2024–2025 generic AI SaaS purple/violet (`#8b5cf6`, `#6D28D9`, `#c4b5fd`, `#a078ff`, `#7c3aed`).
  2. **Zero Plastic AI:** Models must never look like over-smoothed 3D wax dolls. Instead, the course and platform showcase **authentic 24mm iPhone 15 Pro candid photography** (mirror selfies with real camera flash reflection, cozy gamer girls with headsets and controllers, European outdoor cafés in midday sun, pilates reformer studios, golden hour car seats, sunlit kitchens with Venetian blind shadows, and candlelit rooftop dinners—all with deep depth of field, flyaway baby hairs, and natural unretouched skin texture).
- **Character Architecture (Musa vs. Multi-Model Studio):**
  - **Musa (`musa-hero.jpg`, `musa-closeup.jpg`, `musa-rooftop.jpg`, `musa-avatar.jpg`):** Our single, consistent official assistant and host across the Landing Page, WhatsApp Bot, Musa Studio header/support, and Console—plus the flagship case study for 100% facial consistency across angles.
  - **Multi-Model Curriculum (`mod-*.jpg`):** The 13 modules in `academia.html` feature **13 100% distinct cover images** representing the diverse creator niches students learn to build and monetize (`Clara`, `Sofía`, `Valeria`, `Martina`, `Elena`, `Lucía`, `Camila`, `Aitana`, `Nora`, plus Musa's consistency classes).

## 2. Color Palette & Roles (Luminous Non-Dark Champagne & Soft Pastel Rose)

### A. Luminous Editorial Canvas (`index.html`, `academia.html`, `/acceso/:token`)
- **Silk Alabaster (`#FAF7F5`):** Primary non-dark canvas background with a warm champagne-blush undertone; zero eye strain.
- **Gallery Paper White (`#FFFFFF`):** Elevated cards, lookbook frames, and module containers.
- **Whisper Pastel Rose (`#F9EBEE`):** Soft blush surface wash for badges, active highlights, and comparison callouts.
- **Soft Petal Border (`#EBCAD0`):** Delicate structural border for rose-champagne cards and pills.
- **Pastel Dusty Rose (`#D89AA2`):** Secondary accent paired with Champagne Gold for subtle gradients and hover borders.
- **Deep Editorial Rose-Bronze (`#B86B77`):** High-contrast rose accent for eyebrow kickers, station labels, and interactive tags on light backgrounds.
- **Brushed Champagne Gold (`#C8A97E`):** Primary luxury metallic accent.
- **Champagne Bronze (`#9A7240`):** High-contrast warm bronze for technical metadata and secondary icons.
- **Deep Espresso Ink (`#181415`):** Razor-sharp primary typography and dark contrast pills.
- **Warm Taupe (`#5C5352`) & Muted Stone (`#847977`):** Secondary body text and technical captions.
- **Studio Emerald (`#059669`):** Verified completion states, live telemetry indicators, and savings badges.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Display & Editorial Serif (`'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif`):** Used for editorial headlines, italic emphasis (*hiperrealistas*, *Musa Studio*), and high-contrast price display.
- **Primary Geometric Sans (`'Plus Jakarta Sans', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif`):** Clean, modern UI and body copy with tight heading tracking (`-0.025em`).
- **Technical Camera & Telemetry Mono (`'JetBrains Mono', ui-monospace, monospace`):** Used for iPhone/camera metadata (`iPhone 15 Pro · 24mm · Flash Real`), module numbering (`01`–`13`), and live telemetry.

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Buttons (Tactile Champagne-Rose Gold Pills):**
  - Primary CTA: `linear-gradient(135deg, #EAD4B0 0%, #E8C2C8 50%, #C8A97E 100%)` with `#181415` bold Espresso text, top specular highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.85)`), and soft rose-champagne elevation (`0 16px 36px -12px rgba(184, 107, 119, 0.28)`).
- **Cards & Containers:**
  - `16px–20px` radius, `1px solid #E8DFDC` or `#EBCAD0` hairline borders, subtle warm shadows, and smooth `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` hover transitions.
